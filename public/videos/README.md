# EduSync Videos Folder

Adding videos is now super simple:

### Option 1: Just name your videos 1, 2, 3, 4
Drop your `.mp4` video files into this folder:
- `1.mp4` *(for Quick 1: Fractions)*
- `2.mp4` *(for Quick 2: Gravity)*
- `3.mp4` *(for Quick 3: Angles)*
- `4.mp4` *(for Quick 4: Photosynthesis)*

*(Also supports `quick1.mp4`, `quick2.mp4` or `quick-fractions.mp4`)*

---

### Option 2: Instant "Choose File" button on the webpage
Open `http://localhost:5173/` and click the **📁 Choose Local Video** button to select ANY video file from your computer and watch it play instantly!
